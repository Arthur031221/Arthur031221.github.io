SECTION 02 · FOUR THREADS第 02 節 · 四條線

RESEARCH研究

Four threads, one question — what a posterior should carry, and what memory has to do with carrying it.四條研究線，一個問題——後驗應該承載什麼，而記憶與這件事有什麼關係。

L2/3

Predictive coding needs a memory預測編碼需要記憶

Theory理論

Predictive coding gives you a generative hierarchy but no content-addressable memory; modern Hopfield networks give you retrieval but no hierarchy. Both, it turns out, implicitly require the same object — a Bayesian denoiser. Making that precise yields HOPE, a single energy over hierarchical inference, temporal prediction and layer-wise memory, learned with local Hebbian updates alone.預測編碼給你一個生成式階層,但沒有內容定址的記憶;modern Hopfield 網路給你檢索,但沒有階層。結果是,兩者暗中需要的是同一個東西——一個貝氏去噪器。把這件事講精確,就導出 HOPE:一個同時涵蓋階層推論、時間預測與逐層記憶的能量函數,而且只用局部 Hebbian 更新就能學習。

L4

Inference should sample, not just peak推論應該取樣,而不只是找峰

Theory理論

Temporal predictive coding descends the energy deterministically, so it returns only the posterior's MAP mode — and throws away every other explanation the data admits. Pairing overdamped Langevin dynamics to a preconditioner through the fluctuation–dissipation relation makes the filtering posterior the flow's unique stationary density: the inference now carries the whole shape of the belief.時間預測編碼是在能量上做確定性的梯度下降,所以只會回傳後驗的 MAP 峰值——並且丟掉資料容許的其他所有解釋。把 overdamped Langevin 動力學透過漲落–耗散關係與 preconditioner 配對,能讓濾波後驗成為該流的唯一穩態分佈:推論從此帶著信念的完整形狀。

L5a

Reading space out of the brain從大腦裡讀出空間

Neural data神經資料

At the HMI Lab I am building generative decoding models that reconstruct 3D spatial representations from fMRI. The theory above is not decoration here — the same question about what a posterior should carry decides what a decoder is allowed to claim it has recovered.在 HMI Lab,我在建生成式解碼模型,從 fMRI 訊號重建三維空間表徵。上面的理論在這裡不是裝飾——「後驗應該承載什麼」這個問題,直接決定了一個解碼器可以宣稱自己還原了什麼。

L5b

Control without losing the picture在不失去畫面的前提下控制

Generative生成模型

MatrixQR decouples scan reliability from creative editing in QR-code generation, so aesthetics and function stop fighting each other. Langevin dynamics is the mathematical core of score-based diffusion — this line of work and the theory above are the same mathematics wearing different clothes.MatrixQR 把掃描可靠性與創意編輯解耦,讓 QR code 的美觀與功能不再互相打架。Langevin 動力學正是 score-based diffusion 的數學核心——這條線和上面的理論,其實是同一套數學換了身衣服。

L6

Where the threads are written down這些線寫在哪裡

The first two threads are the two co-first-author manuscripts under review; the fourth is MatrixQR at TAAI 2025. The spatial-decoding thread has no paper yet because the work is still running — that gap is real, and it is the next thing.前兩條線就是兩篇共同第一作者、審查中的手稿；第四條是 TAAI 2025 的 MatrixQR。空間解碼那條線目前沒有論文，因為工作仍在進行——這個缺口是真實的，也是下一步。
