Langevin Temporal Predictive Coding Lifts Modes to Multimodal Densities

C.-W. Lee (co-first author) et al.

Preprint · under submission

Replaces the deterministic MAP inference of temporal predictive coding with preconditioned Langevin dynamics. Lifts posterior mode coverage from 19.4% to 71.3% (3.67×) and cuts predictive MAE 16.7% (22.3% on naturally multimodal tasks) over twelve UCI benchmarks.把時間預測編碼的確定性 MAP 推論換成 preconditioned Langevin 動力學。在十二個 UCI benchmark 上,後驗模式涵蓋率從 19.4% 提升到 71.3%(3.67 倍),預測 MAE 降低 16.7%(天生多峰的任務上降 22.3%)。

2026

Predictive Coding with a Sequential Memory-Driven Bayesian Denoiser

C.-W. Lee (co-first author) et al.

Preprint · under submission

A Tweedie–Hopfield correspondence identifies associative memory as the Bayesian denoiser predictive coding implicitly requires, yielding HOPE. Beats predictive-coding and Hopfield baselines on Bouncing Moving-MNIST and UCF-101 recall using only local Hebbian updates — no BPTT, no weight transport.以 Tweedie–Hopfield 對應關係指認出聯想記憶就是預測編碼暗中需要的貝氏去噪器,並由此導出 HOPE。在 Bouncing Moving-MNIST 與 UCF-101 的序列回憶上勝過雙方 baseline,而且只用局部 Hebbian 更新——不需 BPTT,不需權重傳輸。

Under review審查中
