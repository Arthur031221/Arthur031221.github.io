I study how brains and machines represent uncertainty, retrieve memory, and turn noisy evidence into a useful belief.我研究大腦與機器如何表徵不確定性、提取記憶，並把帶有雜訊的證據轉成可用的信念。

In my own words關於我

I graduated from National Tsing Hua University with a double major in Physics and Electrical Engineering & Computer Science (AI Track).

My theory work connects predictive coding, associative memory, and Bayesian inference. Two co-first-author manuscripts are under review: one identifies associative memory as the denoiser predictive coding requires; the other replaces point-estimate inference with preconditioned Langevin sampling.

At NTHU's Human-Centered Machine Intelligence Lab, I use generative models to decode 3D spatial representations from fMRI. In parallel, I have worked on controllable diffusion at Academia Sinica and robustness in chest X-ray classification. I am currently visiting UCLA and looking for NeuroAI PhD positions for 2027 entry.

我畢業於國立清華大學，雙主修物理系與電機資訊學院學士班（AI 組）。

我的理論研究連結預測編碼、聯想記憶與貝氏推論。兩篇共同第一作者手稿正在審查：一篇指出聯想記憶正是預測編碼所需的去噪器；另一篇以 preconditioned Langevin sampling 取代只回傳單一點估計的推論。

在清華 HMI Lab，我以生成模型從 fMRI 解碼三維空間表徵；同時也曾在中研院研究可控 diffusion，並投入胸腔 X 光分類的穩健性問題。目前於 UCLA 訪問，並尋找 2027 年入學的 NeuroAI 博士班機會。
